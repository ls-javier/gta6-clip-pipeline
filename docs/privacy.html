Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

GTA 6 Clips is a private, single-user tool. It has no visitors, no accounts and no analytics or advertising trackers.

Data accessed

When the owner connects their own TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account, the app stores the resulting authorization tokens and basic account identifiers (such as the account ID) on the owner's own computer. It reads the view and like counts of the owner's own posts.

Use and sharing

This data is used only to upload the owner's videos and show the owner their own statistics. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about other people is collected.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and records stay on the owner's computer and can be deleted at any time. The owner can revoke the app's access from each platform's security settings.
